Numeric checks cover promoted boolean arithmetic, aggregate results, nested
push arrays, multiple/mixed/nested device addresses, physical pointer tables,
physical nulls, local pointer selection and finite-allocation pointer loops.
Host boolean math is independently exercised at both optimization levels.

Compile/validation checks cover entry ABI records, resource reference copies
and rebinding, format/type rejection, unknown-format optional features, narrow
storage capabilities, avoidance of unwanted 8-bit features (including padded
float records), volatile reads, stage scope rejection and separate shared/buffer
64-bit atomic requirements. Initialized pointer locals and parameters preserve
alignment facts through aliases; arithmetic and reassignment weaken them.
Unsupported alignment placement is diagnosed rather than silently ignored.

Namespace/global overload catalogs independently cover every applicable name
and check emitted calls to the user's ordinary C++ overload. Statically known
function-pointer calls are resolved before indirect-call restrictions.

Reflection retains schema_version 1 and adds required_limits for push constants,
shared memory, workgroup dimensions and invocation count. Mocked-device tests
check feature/limit acceptance and rejection independently of hardware. Vulkan
execution checks these reflected requirements before creating pipelines.

Limits: resource/descriptor/graphics/ray APIs have compile and SPIR-V validation
coverage, not texture/rendering/traversal execution coverage. Advanced stages,
inline assembly, the Slang corpus and packaging remain pending. Arbitrary
reinterpretation, dynamic local record-field offsets and local pointer escapes
remain unsupported; do not interpret these results as coverage of that work.
